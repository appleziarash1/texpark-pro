PC + phone ek shathe — data hariye jabe na

Ei panch ta jinis thik kora hoyeche, tai duto machine ekshathe chalaleo hishab guliye jabe na, ar kokhono ekta machine onno tar kaj muchhe felbe na.

🔒Memo number ar clash korbe na

Age PC ar phone dutoi TXP/SM/<tarikh>-001 banato, ar sheet oi number diyei save kore — tai ek memo arek tar upor likhe jeto. Ekhon number-e device tag thake:

  • PC: TXP/SM/2026/09/22-PC001
  • Phone: TXP/SM/2026/09/22-PH001
  • Purchase number-o same niyome (PC/PH)
  • Tag blank thakle nijei PC/PH dhore ney

♻Sync ekhon verify kore, ar ferot-o ane

Age sync shudhu pathato — response pora jeto na, tai "pathano hoyeche" mane "save hoyeche" chilo na. Ar kichu ferot anar upay chilo na.

  • Response na pele "synced" bole na
  • Internet na thakle queue-te thake, firlo nijei pathay
  • Cloud theke restore kora jay

⌛Nijei sync kore — 30 second por por notun

Age PC-te ekta memo likhle phone-e seta dekhte app-ta bondho kore abar khulte hoto. Ekhon app khola thakle prottek 30 second-e nijei notun data tene ney.

  • App khola thakle 30 sec por por auto-pull
  • Notun kichu na pele screen ekbar-o nosto hoy na — apni jekhane type korchen sekhanei thaken
  • App chhoto korle poll bondho — background-e data kharoch kore na
  • Android app-e-o same — PC-te likhle phone-e nijei chole ashe

🌐Company, settings, user — sob sync hoy notun

Age shudhu memo ar stock jeto. Company-r nam, memo prefix, low-stock level, ar staff-er login — egulo ek machine-e set kore onno machine-e abar korte hoto.

  • Company details (nam, phone, address, BIN, VAT) ek jaygay set korlei sob jaygay
  • Notun user PC-te banale phone-e-o login kora jay
  • Password PC-te bodlale phone-e-o kaj kore
  • Jetar value notun, seta-i jite jay — purono ta notun ta muchhe fele na

📱Prottek device-er nijer setting alada thake notun

Sync URL, device tag, ar auto-pull — ei tinta ek device theke onno device-e jay na. Age egulo sync hole phone PC-er tag niye nito, ar phone-er memo number PC-er sathe mishe jeto.

  • Sync URL sync hoy na — ekta device-er URL onno device-e jete pare na
  • Device tag (PC/PH) nijer ta-i thake — memo number clash kore na
  • Auto-pull on/off prottek device-e alada
  • Baki sob business setting sync hoy — data hariye jay na

⚠Sync bondho thakle Dashboard-ei bole dey notun

Ekta device jodi ekhono sheet-er sathe joda na lage, tahole Dashboard ta notun dokan-er moto sob 0 dekhate pare — ar mone hoy data hariye geche. Ekhon seta chup thake na: page-er upore-i ekta lal-caution banner bole dey je ei device-er data onno jaygay jacche na, ar kivabe thik korben.

  • Dashboard-ei spashto warning — kono device onno device-er data dekhche na
  • Bole dey screen-e ja ache seta shudhu demo product, apnar data na
  • Pairing link — PC-te ekbar URL boshie link banan, phone-e khullei phone nijei joda lage
  • Native Android app-e-o same warning

📈Purono memo-r bhul profit thik korun notun

Memo save howar shomoy tar buying price memo-te lekha hoye jay. Tai age jodi kono memo bhul cost niye lekha hoye thake, tahole product-e dam thik korleo sei purono memo-r profit ar nijei thik hoy na. Ekhon Backup / Data page theke ek click-e seta thik kora jay.

  • Age dekhay koto gulo memo bhul, ar purono profit vs thik profit

  • Shudhu profit ar cost thik kore — bikri, qty, due, stock kichui bodlabe na
  • Dobar chalale kichu badlay na — double-correction hoy na
