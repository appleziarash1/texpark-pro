Cloud sync (Firestore) Cloud off (Sheet only)

The Google Sheet above ships the whole book and is not instant. This adds live sync: one changed memo is one small update, so a sale typed on the phone shows on the PC in seconds. It uses Firebase's free plan — no card, no server. If the cloud is unreachable, or over quota, the app keeps saving and falls back to the Sheet by itself, then recovers when the cloud comes back. Leave it empty and nothing changes.

Backup & Restore

Automatic safety copy: a snapshot is kept before every save, so a wrong entry can be undone.

Fix old memos’ profit

When a memo is saved, its buying price (cost) is written into the memo. If a memo was saved with a wrong buying price, fixing the product's price does not fix that old memo's profit. This button looks through the old memos, shows how many are wrong, and then — with your approval — recalculates them using the product's current buying price.

What changes: only profit and cost of goods. Sales, customer, qty and due are untouched. Stock is untouched too.

Import from old app (v1)

Data from the old app (texpark_biz_v1) can be brought in here if it is on this PC. The old key is not deleted — it is only read and mapped to the new model. Old memos had no cost, so their profit shows as 0.
