RECEIVABLE + DELIVERY COLLECTION (ki notun holo)
================================================

Ki thik holo (main kaj):
  Ager obosthay memo-r "Due" ekbar likhe dile ar palte na. Customer delivery-r
  shomoy taka dileo dashboard-er "Receivable" (mot due) ar memo-r due ekhono
  purono onko-i dekhato. Tai hisab milto na.

  Ekhon delivery korar shomoy ekta "Collected (৳)" box ache. Customer delivery-r
  shomoy jodi kono taka dey, shei taka oi box-e likhe Save Delivery chap dile
  sei taka memo-r due theke shathe shathe komে jabe. Ar dashboard-er Receivable
  o, history-r due o, customer ledger o, ageing (Due >60d) o — shob ek shathe
  thik hoye jabe.

Box ta nijei bhorti hoye ashe:
  - Pura memo deliver hole box pura due dekhabe.
  - Adha deliver hole (jemon 10 er 4) box due-r shei ongsho (10-er 4 vag) dekhabe.
  - Apni chailে box-er onko bondole dite paren, ba 0 rekhe dite paren (jodi
    ei trip-e taka na dey).

Niom gulo (guarantee):
  - Jodi apni due-r beshi taka likhen, system apnake janabe ar
    shudhu joto due ache toto-i record korbe. Kono due kokhono negative hobe na.
  - Receipt ar delivery EK chhe kobe save hoy (ek commit-e). Tai delivery hole
    taka mochh kore jabe na, ar taka record hoye delivery harie jabe na.
  - Jodi khali memo teh kono receipt na thake, tahole due ager motoi thake —
    purono kono onko bondol hobe na. Purono data ekdom ager motoi pora jabe.

Receipt share kora:
  - Delivery-r receipt ta memo ar delivery dutor shathe juda thake (memoId,
    deliveryId). Tai memo delete korle shei receipt o shathe chole jay —
  kono taka "orphan" pore thake na.
  - Abar ek bar Save chap dile (double click) notun delivery/receipt toiri
    hobe na.
  - "Receive" button (purono system) age-r motoi kaj kore: customer ledger-e
    dhukbe. Dashboard to memo-r upor nirbhor kore, tai oikhanে purono hisab
    ekdom same-i thake.

Google Sheet-er dike (apps script):
  - Payment row guলো ekhon "Payment ID" diye upsert hoy. Ek ta receipt ekbar-
    er beshi pathale (retry / sync replay) notun row toiri hobe na, purono ta
    update hobe. Payment ID na thakle purono motoi notun row hoy.
  - Payments tab purono ar chhoto (6 column) holeo code age column boro kore
    ney, tai "Those rows/columns are out of bounds." ar asbe na.
