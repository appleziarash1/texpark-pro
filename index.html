⚠ Ekta real somossa pelam — ei ta age thik kora dorkar

Apnar app-e sync-er khetre ekta silent data-loss risk ache:

  • pushSync() mode:'no-cors' diye pathay — mane browser response porte pare na. Tai "✓ Sync request sent" lekha ta asol-e save hoyeche kina jane na. Google bhor kore fail korleo app "✓" dekhabe.
  • Apnar Code.gs-er nijer comment-ei ei durbalta lekha ache: "...even if the separate memo request is interrupted by Electron closing/clearing the memo immediately after save." — mane ei somossa apnara age onubhob korechen, ar ekta workaround diyechen.
  • Kono retry / offline queue nei — internet na thakle oi memo Sheet-e kokhono jabe na, kintu app bole "sent".

Amar poramorsho: upgrade-e shudhu feature add na, ei sync ta asol-e verify kora + retry queue + "Failed → Pending → Synced" status dibo. Tahole apni nishchinte bhorosa korte parben.

Amar poramorsho (honest suggestion)

Ekhon apni jodi "profit" chaan — tahole apnar dorkar ta Option A — Texpark Pro. Karon:

  1. Apnar ekhonkar app-e profit shobdo-i nai — mane cost track hochche na. Ei ta shobcheye boro gap.
  2. A option apnar existing 9 ta feature ektaw nosto kore na, shudhu add kore.
  3. Sync-er silent-fail somossa taw thik hoy — ei ta apnar asol business risk.

Kintu jodi mone hoy app-ta age thik kora dorkar — tahole E — Stabilized Lite (2–3 din) diye shuru korben, tarpor A te jaben. Ei dui step-er path ta shobcheye safe.
